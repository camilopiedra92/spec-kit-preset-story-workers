**What was built**, in `src/checkout/`:
- **Pricing with tax:** the catalog, the tax table, the cart (quantities 1–99, one line per SKU, kept in the order first added), and per-line tax rounded half-even. Order amounts are the sums of the line amounts.
- **Promotions:** buy X get Y free, percentage off and fixed amount off, applied in the spec's stacking order. The discount cap and the fixed amount are split across lines by largest remainder. Exclusive promotions follow the spec's tie-break rules.
