**Spec ambiguities the reviews raised.** The spec's wording doesn't decide these, so they don't count as failures:
- **Curly apostrophe:** a typographic apostrophe (`’`, U+2019) splits a word. `count_words("don’t")` gives 2, while `"don't"` gives 1.
- **Number symbols:** fractions and Roman numerals count as words. `count_words("½")` gives 1.
- **Combining marks:** accent marks typed as separate characters split words. Hindi `"हिन्दी"` counts as 3 words instead of 1.
- **ß:** `top_words` lower-cases with `.lower()`, so `"Straße"` and `"STRASSE"` are counted as different words.
- **Sort order:** ties are sorted by raw character code, so accented words sort after `z`.

If any of these should behave differently, the spec needs to say so first, for example through `/speckit-clarify`.
